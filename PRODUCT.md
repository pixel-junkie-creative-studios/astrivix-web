Success is measured in sub-second performance, high conversion rates, direct client acquisition, and bulletproof codebase delivery.

## Brand Commitments

Authoritative, precise, immaculate. Astrivix Corp speaks with executive clarity and engineering confidence:
- **Direct**: Concrete capabilities, verifiable specs, transparent workflows.
- **Specific**: Exact metrics, real client results, clear project deliverables.
- **Craft-Led**: Custom code, bespoke typography, dark mineral aesthetics, zero AI-slop visual clichés.

Three-word personality: **elite, decisive, immaculate**.

Avoid:
- **AI-Slop Marketing**: Generic purple-to-blue gradients, neon glow trails, floating pill badges above every title, emojis in headings.
- **SaaS Clichés**: Cards nested in cards, 3 identical icon boxes in a row, low-contrast gray text on dark backgrounds.
- **Buzzword Copy**: "Cutting-edge", "game-changing", "butter-smooth", "120 FPS".

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Cliché**: Every interface element, border, and line of code earns its place.
2. **High-Contrast Readability**: Dark lacquer surfaces paired with crisp neutral text and gold/verdigris accents for 100% legibility.
3. **Purposeful Motion**: Snappy, staggered entrance transitions with spring physics; zero repetitive scroll lag.
4. **Single H1 Semantic Hierarchy**: Strict single `<h1>` heading per page route for maximum SEO authority.
5. **Full Transparency**: Direct communication channels, instant project intake, zero hidden platform locks.
